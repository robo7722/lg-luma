# UI sounds

`assets/sounds/*.wav` (focus, select, back, bump) were generated with
ElevenLabs Sound Effects (eleven_text_to_sound_v2) for this fork and then
trimmed, layered, pitched and level-matched by hand. Format: 48 kHz, mono,
16-bit PCM WAV.

`assets/sounds/retro-*.wav` (Retro TV blips and tape tracking noise) were generated
with ElevenLabs Sound Effects (eleven_text_to_sound_v2) and trimmed and level-matched.
`assets/sounds/retro-music.wav` was generated with ElevenLabs Music (music_v1), downmixed
to 24 kHz mono and cut into a beat-exact 18-bar loop. `assets/sounds/robos-boot.wav`
(RobOS logo sound for the design switch) was generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects with an
added echo tail. Sources: `tools/sound-design/retro/`.
